How to reset TVB-OA user's password.

Reset Corporate User ID Password

TVB-OA / TVBI-OA — Password Change Procedure

Password Requirements

  • At least 8 characters containing letters (upper & lower case) and numbers — e.g. Tmchan99
  • Must not contain your first name, last name, or login name
  • Password history = 1 — the last password cannot be reused
  • Password expires every 365 days

Option I TVB-IT computer

For TVB-OA / TVBI-OA network users on a TVB-IT computer.

  1. Press CTRL ALT DELETE
  2. Click “Change a password”
  3. Enter your existing password in the “Old password” box
  4. Enter the new password in “New password”, and again in “Confirm password”
  5. Press Enter
  6. Reboot your computer for the change to take effect.
Alternatively, use the self-service password-change website (see Option III address).

Option II ENG / NMG off-network

For ENG or NMG users not joined to the TVB-IT network.

  1. Go to:
    https://winpwd.tvb-oa.tvb.org.hk/RDWeb/Pages/en-US/password.aspx
  2. Enter your domain login ID in the “Domain\user account” box, e.g. TVB-OA\AB12345 or TVBI-OA\AB12345
  3. Enter your existing password in the “Current password” box
  4. Enter the new password in “New password”, and again in “Confirm new password”
  5. Click “Submit”
  6. Reboot your computer for the change to take effect.

Option III External users

For external users (non-TVB group domain network).

  1. Go to:
    https://103.84.222.22/RDWeb/Pages/en-US/password.aspx
  2. Enter your domain login ID in the “Domain\user account” box
  3. Enter your existing password in the “Current password” box
  4. Enter the new password in “New password”, and again in “Confirm new password”
  5. Click “Submit”
  6. Reboot your computer for the change to take effect.
